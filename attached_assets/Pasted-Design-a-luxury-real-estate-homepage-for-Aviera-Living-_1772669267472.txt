Design a luxury real estate homepage for “Aviera Living” 
strictly aligned with the official brand identity and company profile.

The website must reflect:
• Legal precision
• Transparency
• Boutique luxury
• Founder-led expertise
• Trust over transaction volume

The feeling must be calm, refined, editorial, and confident — never flashy.

==================================================
BRAND COLOR SYSTEM (STRICT)
==================================================

Main Colors:
• Cacao — #3D2716 (Primary brand base)
• Forest Green — #424D38
• Stone — #D8BFAE

Secondary Colors:
• Terra Cotta — #995134
• Sand — #917C63
• Pearl — #FAF8F5

Usage Rules:
• Background base: Pearl or Stone
• Hero overlay: Cacao (80% opacity)
• Section contrast blocks: Forest Green
• Buttons: Cacao background / Pearl text
• Accent hover: Terra Cotta
• Avoid bright gold gradients (brand is earthy, not flashy)

==================================================
TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM (MANDATORY)
==================================================

HEADINGS:
• Le Jour Serif
• Symphony (for large display titles & hero)

Style:
• Large editorial spacing
• High contrast serif elegance
• Uppercase for navigation
• Slight letter spacing

BODY TEXT:
• Inria Serif
• Used for:
  - Paragraphs
  - Descriptions
  - Modal links
  - Social captions
  - Buttons
  - UI text

Typography tone:
Calm. Legal. Assured. Refined.
